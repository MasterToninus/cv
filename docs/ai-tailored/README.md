Historical facts and review limits remain tied to their recorded dates.

The archived notes describe their original review dates. Later author-confirmed
corrections are recorded in [the current shared-data notes](../confirmed-data.md);
do not silently replace the original answers with subsequent facts. Application
policy reviews remain separately organized under
[`AI-policy-disclosure/reviews/`](../AI-policy-disclosure/reviews/).
